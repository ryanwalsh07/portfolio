IMAGES
======

The case studies are on hold, so their images have been removed and the home
page cards use a CSS placeholder instead. The only image the live site expects
is the sharing preview.

SHARING PREVIEW (the card shown when your link is pasted into LinkedIn, Slack etc.)
  share-card.png           1200 x 630.

Names are case sensitive. "Share-Card.PNG" will not work; "share-card.png" will.

When the case studies return, restore the case study pages and card markup, and
add their images here. Keep each file under about 1 MB.
